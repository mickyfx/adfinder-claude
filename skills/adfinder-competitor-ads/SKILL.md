Read [the tool-use guide](../../TOOL-CONTRACT.md) before using AdFinder tools.

Identify the requested brands, platform, country and question. Prioritize the available Meta, TikTok, Google and LinkedIn ad libraries. Do not describe organic X/Twitter or Reddit posts as ad-library results. Compatible ad-library links may identify an advertiser; arbitrary feed links are not guaranteed to identify an ad.

Discover the relevant registered operation and inspect its schema. Preserve the requested source and use only supported filters. Quote the bounded search and obtain a credit cap before execution. Follow the shared request/budget and uncertain-retry rules in the guide. Do not keep paginating beyond the agreed scope.

Report advertiser, source platform, reported status, dates, ad text, format, available placements and original links when returned. Missing placement, device, performance or other fields remain unavailable. Separate retrieved facts from messaging interpretations. Explain sample limits and distinguish ad counts from creative counts. Display the completed saved result ONCE using the guide’s free display flow, with separate ad/creative navigation; never run a new search just to render it. Comparison is the host assistant’s analysis of retrieved data, not a separate paid analysis call.
