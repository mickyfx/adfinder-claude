description: Diagnose AdFinder sign-in, credit balance, subscription or result-display problems without executing paid research.
---

Read [the tool-use guide](../../TOOL-CONTRACT.md) before using AdFinder tools.

Identify the affected host, authenticated account and observed error. Distinguish package installation, OAuth connection, server balance and payment fulfillment. Use exposed free balance/discovery/description tools only as relevant; never execute paid research as a troubleshooting test. Do not request passwords, tokens, card data or full receipts in chat.

Show balance.remaining and returned buckets; show reviewBonus separately and exclude it from research funds when spendableViaMcp is false. Missing values remain unknown. For a free plan, allowance is the one-time grant; for pro it is monthly subscription-credit allocation, not a wallet ceiling. Annual subscriptions also allocate monthly: allocation/reset dates do not prove billing cadence or renewal. Use explicit purchase-channel metadata for billing details; otherwise leave them unknown.

Use the host connection UI for expired/revoked access. A checkout redirect or pending payment does not prove credits were granted; do not advise another purchase while payment is uncertain. Do not merge accounts, switch identities, grant credits or invent refund/deletion tools. Initial free credits are one-time, subscription credits do not roll over, and purchased credits do not expire. Mobile Collections remain local.

For presentation issues, reuse the original completed request through the guide’s free display flow; do not create new execution IDs or claim rendering from tool JSON. Provide a concise non-secret support summary and https://adfinder.app/plugin-support.html if unresolved. Do not send messages without explicit authorization.
